## About This Site

This is my corner of the web where I write about anything I find interesting. You'll find:
- **Reviews** of products I actually use (Amazon purchases, tech gadgets, etc.)
- **Guides** for features or tools that aren't well-documented elsewhere
- **Random thoughts** on security, privacy, tech, or whatever catches my attention

Everything here is **comprehensive** and written in [layman's terms](https://www.laymansterms.com/what-does-laymans-terms-mean), and gets straight to the point. No ads either! However, if I ever get sponsored for something, I'll be transparent about it in my post.

## What You Can Expect

I'm also a power user who enjoys digging into niche features and undocumented stuff. If I discover something useful that isn't widely known, I'll probably write about it here. My reviews are honest and based on personal experience.
