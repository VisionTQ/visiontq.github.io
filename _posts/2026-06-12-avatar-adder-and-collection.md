> Make sure your NPSSO cookie is fresh before using PSNTools. Sign out of PSN and sign back in if needed. [NPSSO guide here](/posts/how-to-get-your-npsso-token-for-playstation-api-access/).
{: .prompt-info }

![password during checkout](/assets/psn-tools/avatar-adder-disable-password-checkout.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

Before you start, make sure **password during checkout** is disabled in your PSN account settings. You can change this under your PlayStation account settings.

> If you don't disable it, the checkout process will keep asking you to enter your password every time and will fail.
{: .prompt-warning }

---

## Step 1: Go to Avatar Adder & Collection

On the PSNTools main page, look for the **Avatar** **Adder** & **Collection** section. Click it

![PSNTools main page showing Avatar, Adder, Collection section](/assets/psn-tools/avatar-adder-main-page.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

---

## Step 2: Add an Item to Your Cart

2. Once you are in the section, choose an avatar or item & click **Add to Cart**.
3.  After adding items, click the cart icon in the top right corner to review your selection.
4.  A pop-up will appear showing your cart. Click **Proceed to Checkout** to move forward.

![Add avatar to cart and click the cart icon in the top right corner](/assets/psn-tools/avatar-adder-add-to-cart.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

---




---

## Step 5: Checkout Your Cart

5. On the next page, click **Checkout Cart** to complete the purchase. Make sure you have sufficient wallet funds in your PSN account.

![Pop-up appears, click proceed to checkout](/assets/psn-tools/avatar-adder-proceed-checkout.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

Make sure **password during checkout** is disabled in your PSN settings before doing this, or the checkout will fail.
