## Step 1: Sign Out & Back Into PlayStation

Go to [PlayStation.com](https://www.playstation.com). Click your profile icon in the top right corner.

![PlayStation.com profile icon in top right corner](/assets/psn-tools/playstation-profile-corner.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

From the dropdown, go to your account section & click **Sign Out**.

![PlayStation account section, click sign out](/assets/psn-tools/playstation-account-sign-out.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

Once signed out, **sign back in** with your PSN account.

> Signing out and back in gives you a fresh cookie that PSNTools needs to authenticate you.
{: .prompt-info }

---

## Step 2: Sign Up on PSNTools

Go to the [PSNTools sign-up page](https://www.psntools.com/auth/register).

![PSNTools sign-up page](/assets/psn-tools/psn-tools-signup-new.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

---

## Step 3: Paste Your NPSSO Cookie

On the sign-up page, click the **NPSSO** bar. (Make sure youre logged into PSN first), then click it.

![PSNTools sign-up, click the NPSSO bar](/assets/psn-tools/npsso-bar-click.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

This will open a page showing your cookie. **Copy only the text between the quotes**, not the whole page.

![NPSSO cookie, copy only between the quotes](/assets/psn-tools/npsso-cookie-copy-between-quotes.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

Paste that cookie back into the sign-up form on PSNTools.

![Paste the NPSSO cookie into the sign-up form](/assets/psn-tools/paste-npsso-cookie-new.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

Choose a password. It should **not** be the same as your PSN account password. This is your PSNTools account password.

![PSNTools, set a new password (not your PSN password)](/assets/psn-tools/psn-tools-password-field.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

> If you slready have an account? Click **Yes, update account**.
{: .prompt-info }

---

## Step 4: Verify Your Email

After signing up, you'll be taken to the email verification section. Enter your PlayStation account email and click **Resend**.

![PSNTools email verification, click Resend](/assets/psn-tools/email-verification-resend.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

Go to your inbox and look for the PSNTools verification email (check spam if you don't see it). Click **Verify Account**.

![PSNTools verification email in inbox, click Verify Account](/assets/psn-tools/verification-email-inbox.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

> Link expires in 60 minutes. Check spam if you don't see it.
{: .prompt-warning }

---

## Step 5: All Done

Your dashboard is ready. Manage **Profile**, **Membership**, & **PSN Authentication** from here.

![PSNTools account dashboard](/assets/psn-tools/account-dashboard.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }
