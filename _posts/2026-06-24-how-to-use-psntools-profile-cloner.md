> Make sure your NPSSO cookie is fresh before using PSNTools. Sign out of PSN and sign back in if needed. [NPSSO guide here](/posts/how-to-get-your-npsso-token-for-playstation-api-access/).
{: .prompt-info }

## Step 1: Go to PSN Profile Cloner

Head over to the [PSNTools homepage](https://www.psntools.com) and find the **PSN Profile Cloner** section. Click it to get started.

![PSNTools homepage with PSN Profile Cloner highlighted](/assets/psn-tools/psn-cloner-landing.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

---

## Step 2: Search for a PlayStation Profile

After clicking PSN Profile Cloner, you will see a search bar. Enter the PSN ID of any PlayStation profile you want to clone and hit search. It will show you the account that matched your search.

![PSN Profile Cloner search bar](/assets/psn-tools/psn-cloner-search.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

---

## Step 3: Copy Their Background Color

Once the profile loads, you can choose to copy their **profile background color**. This will apply their exact background color to your own profile.

> This feature only works on PS4, not PS5.
{: .prompt-warning }

![PSN Profile Cloner, copy background color option](/assets/psn-tools/psn-cloner-background-color.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

---

## Step 4: Copy Their Avatar

You can also copy their **profile avatar**. This will set your avatar to match theirs. This feature is subject to availability and may require a membership.

![PSN Profile Cloner, copy avatar option](/assets/psn-tools/psn-cloner-avatar.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

---

## Step 5: Copy Their About Me

Finally, you can copy their **about me** or description section. This will clone whatever text, symbols, or description they have on their PlayStation profile to your own.

![PSN Profile Cloner, copy about me / description option](/assets/psn-tools/psn-cloner-about-me.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

---
