Profile Modding lets you use the profile changes you bought for your own PSN account.

> **Before you start:** Complete [Service access](/posts/psntools-self-service-service-access/) first, including connecting your PSN account.

## Step 1: Open Profile Customization

**If you logged in with your order details:**

1. On the **Your Services** page, find the **Profile Customization** card.
2. Click it.

**If you used the magic link:**

1. Open the magic link from your order.
2. On the **Your Services** page, click **Profile Customization**.

![Your Services page with the Profile Customization card highlighted](/assets/psn-tools/self-service/profile-modding-open.png)

## Step 2: Choose what to change

![Profile Customization page with About Me, Real Name, Background Color, and Background Image](/assets/psn-tools/self-service/profile-modding-page.png)

- **About Me:** Set your bio with custom PS5 icons, up to 140 characters.
- **Real Name:** Use special characters, or fill or hide your real name.
- **Background Color:** Pick a quick color or enter a hex code.
- **Background Image:** Upload a PNG, JPEG, or GIF up to 10 MB.

Each section shows how many changes you have left. Click **Update** in a section to use one.

> **Note:** Background color only works on PS4. The feature does not exist on PS5.
