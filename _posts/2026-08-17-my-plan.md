The plan is built around CS. A master's abroad is the end goal, since undergrad transfers abroad is complex, there are English-taught CS and AI master's programs abroad with KASP covering graduate degrees. KFUPM and KSU both have official transfer routes for CS and AI.

## The plan in three lines

1. Change the major to CS at UQU, build a high GPA.
2. Transfer to KFUPM or KSU for CS/AI.
3. After the bachelor's, do a master's abroad.

## The series

Each step has its own post with the official requirements:

1. **Changing the major to CS at UQU**: UQU's internal transfer rules and what the CS college requires.
2. **Transferring to KFUPM or KSU for CS/AI**: the official requirements for both universities, the documents, and the application windows.
3. **A master's abroad**: English-taught CS/AI programs, KASP, and costs.
