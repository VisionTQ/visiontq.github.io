## Step 1: Go to PSNTools

Head over to the [PSNTools homepage](https://www.psntools.com).

![PSNTools main page](/assets/psn-tools/password-change-psntools-homepage.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

---

## Step 2: Log Out of Your Account

Click on your PSN avatar in the top right corner, then click **Log Out**.

![PSNTools, click avatar and log out](/assets/psn-tools/password-change-logout.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

---

## Step 3: Click Forgot Your Password

Go to the login page and click the **Forgot your password?** link.

![PSNTools login page with forgot password link highlighted](/assets/psn-tools/password-change-forgot-password.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

---

## Step 4: Enter Your Email

Enter the email address associated with your PSNTools account and submit.

![PSNTools forgot password, email input field](/assets/psn-tools/password-change-email-input.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

---

## Step 5: Check Your Email

You will receive a password reset email. Open it and click the **Reset Password** link.

![Password reset email from PSNTools](/assets/psn-tools/password-change-reset-email.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

---

## Step 6: Set Your New Password

Write your new password, confirm it, and you're done.

![Set new password page](/assets/psn-tools/password-change-new-password.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

---

If you don't have a PSNTools account yet, check out the guide on [how to create one](/posts/how-to-create-a-psn-tools-account-short).
