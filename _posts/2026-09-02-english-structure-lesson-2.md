We took a **revision** from our previous lecture about:

## Do / Does in the Simple Present

|     Auxiliary      | Used with        | Verb form     |
| :----------------: | :--------------- | :------------ |
|   **Do** / don't   | I, we, you, they | verb only     |
| **Does** / doesn't | he, she, it      | verb + **-s** |

### Examples

- I **do** my homework every day.
- You **do not** (don't) eat meat.
- We **do** speak English in class.
- They **do not** (don't) live here.
- He **does** his work on time.
- She **does not** (doesn't) like coffee.
- It **does** rain a lot in winter.

> **Rule:** Use **does** (not *do*) with **he, she, it**, and add **-s** to the main verb.
{: .prompt-tip }

## Yes / No Questions

- **Do** you like tea? → Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
- **Does** she play football? → Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.

## WH Questions

| Question word | Use | Example |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| What | things | **What** do you study? |
| Where | places | **Where** does he live? |
| When | time | **When** do they arrive? |
| Who | people | **Who** does she meet? |
| Why | reason | **Why** don't you come? |
| How | manner | **How** do you go to school? |
