## What Is PSN Checker?

PSN Checker is a tool on [PSNTools](https://www.psntools.com) that lets you look up PlayStation accounts. You can search by username or account ID & get details like trophies, region, friends list, online ID history, & more.

If you haven't made a PSNTools account yet, check out the guide on [how to create one](/posts/how-to-create-a-psn-tools-account-short).

---

## Step 1: Go to PSN Checker

Head over to the [PSN Checker page](https://www.psntools.com/psn/checker).

![PSN Checker main page](/assets/psn-tools/psn-checker-main-page.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

---

## Step 2: Enable Advanced Mode

On the checker page, click or toggle **Advanced Mode**. Once enabled, it will default to **NP Online mode**

![PSN Checker, enable advanced mode](/assets/psn-tools/psn-checker-advanced-mode.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

---

## Step 3: Choose Your Search Type

If you have a [PSNTools subscription](https://www.psntools.com/memberships), you get more search options. You can look up accounts by:

- Account ID
- Hex
- Base64
- SHA-256

Without a subscription, you can still search by username  just fine.

![PSN Checker search type options](/assets/psn-tools/psn-checker-search-options.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

---

## Step 4: Search for an Account

Type in the username or account ID you want to look up & hit search.

![PSN Checker, enter username or account ID](/assets/psn-tools/psn-checker-search-input.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

---

## Step 5: Read the Results

After searching, you will see the account's details. This includes:

- Username (online ID)
- First & last name
- Online ID history (shows if the user has ever changed their ID)
- Region
- Trophies
- Friends list
- Account level

![PSN Checker results, account info shown](/assets/psn-tools/psn-checker-results.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

---

## Free vs Paid

With the free version of PSNTools, you can see the basics like the region, levels, trophies, friends list, & first & last name.

If you buy a [subscription](https://www.psntools.com/memberships), you unlock a lot more like advanced search types, deeper account data, & other tools across the site.
