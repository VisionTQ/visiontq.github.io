> Make sure your NPSSO cookie is fresh before using PSNTools. Sign out of PSN and sign back in if needed. [NPSSO guide here](/posts/how-to-get-your-npsso-token-for-playstation-api-access/).

## Step 1: Go to the Avatar Finder
Head over to the [Avatar Finder page](https://www.psntools.com/psn/avatar/finder). Enter your PSN account name in the search bar.

![PSN Avatar Name Finder page with search bar](/assets/psn-tools/avatar-finder-page.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

---

## Step 2: Search for an Avatar Name

After the results load, you will see a the avatar of the PSN ID. Click the **Search Avatar Name** bar.

![Avatar Finder results with username highlighted and search bar](/assets/psn-tools/avatar-finder-results.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

---

## Step 3: View Avatar Details

After clicking search, you can see all the avatar information, including:
- Avatar publisher
- Supported platforms
- Regions
- Image resolution & formats
- Which users can see which avatars on their PSN

![Avatar Finder details showing publisher, platforms, regions, and more](/assets/psn-tools/avatar-finder-details.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }
