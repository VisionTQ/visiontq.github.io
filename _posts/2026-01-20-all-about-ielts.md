## What is the IELTS test?

It stands for **I**nternational **E**nglish **L**anguage **T**esting **S**ystem and it's for anyone who wants to study abroad or work in an English Speaking country

### The 3 Types of the test:

- **IELTS Academic**: For People who want to study abroad in an English-speaking country
- **IELTS General Training**: For people who wants to work or migrate to an English-speaking country
- **Finally IELTS UKVI**: For UK Visas

For me however if im going to study abroad then i would choose **IELTS Academic.**

### IELTS test formats
- Paper
- Computer
- or Online

For **Paper** and **Computer**, they are conducted in IELTS test centers around the world.
Also, for the IELTS Paper test you write the answers by hand, but for the computer-based test you type your answers using the computer. They are both **face-to-face** & the content is the same for both as well.

## The 4 Sections
- Listening
