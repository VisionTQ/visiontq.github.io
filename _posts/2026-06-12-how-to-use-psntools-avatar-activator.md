> Make sure your NPSSO cookie is fresh before using PSNTools. Sign out of PSN and sign back in if needed. [NPSSO guide here](/posts/how-to-get-your-npsso-token-for-playstation-api-access/).
{: .prompt-info }

## Step 1: Go to Avatar Activator
Head over to the [Avatar Activator page](https://www.psntools.com/psn/avatar-activator). Or find the **Avatar Activator** section and click it. This will take you to the activator page.

![PSNTools Avatar Activator section on main page](/assets/psn-tools/avatar-activator-section.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

---

## Step 2: Sign In

On the activator page, click **Sign In**.

![Avatar Activator, click sign in](/assets/psn-tools/avatar-activator-sign-in.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

---

## Step 3: Choose an Avatar

You will see a catalog of avatars to choose from. Pick the one you want to activate on your account. Then Click Activate.

![Avatar Activator catalog, choose an avatar](/assets/psn-tools/avatar-activator-catalog.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

---

## Step 4: Avatar Activated

A pop-up will appear saying **Avatar activated successfully**. In the same pop-up, click the **Verify Profile** button.

![Avatar activated successfully pop-up with verify button](/assets/psn-tools/avatar-activator-success.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

---

## Step 5: View Your Profile

After clicking verify, you can see your account information & the avatar you just activated.

![Avatar Activator, account information with activated avatar](/assets/psn-tools/avatar-activator-verify.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

---

If you don't have a PSNTools account yet, check out the guide on [how to create one](/posts/how-to-create-a-psn-tools-account-short).
