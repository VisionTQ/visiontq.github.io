Avatar Adding lets you use the avatar unlocks you bought for your own PSN account.

> **Before you start:** Complete [Service access](/posts/psntools-self-service-service-access/) first, including connecting your PSN account.

## Step 1: Open Avatars

**If you logged in with your order details:**

1. On the **Your Services** page, find the **Avatars** card.
2. Click it.

**If you used the magic link:**

1. Open the magic link from your order.
2. On the **Your Services** page, click **Avatars**.

![Your Services page with the Avatars card highlighted](/assets/psn-tools/self-service/avatar-adding-open.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

## Step 2: Choose how many avatars to add

![Avatars page with the Avatar Entitlement and Purchase Avatars sections](/assets/psn-tools/self-service/avatar-adding-page.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

The **Avatar Entitlement** section shows how many avatars you have left. For example, 400 remaining.

1. In **Purchase Avatars**, choose a quantity.
2. You can add up to **50 avatars per day**.
3. Click **Purchase Avatar**.

The avatars are unlocked on your account within a minute.

> **Note:** The quantity cannot be higher than your remaining entitlement or the daily limit of 50.
