## Software and Built-in Features

These features come from the official user guide and PC Manager.

### Performance Mode (Fn + P)

![Performance Mode indicator](/assets/img/laptop/matebook-d16-performance-mode.png){: .shadow .rounded-10 }

Performance Mode boosts CPU and GPU performance but:
- Requires the laptop to be plugged in and have at least 20% battery
- Automatically disables when unplugged, battery low, or laptop restarted
- Increases fan noise and heat (this is normal)

> Press **Fn + P** to toggle. An on-screen indicator confirms whether it's on or off. You can also enable it from inside PC Manager.

### Keyboard Backlight

The keyboard has a backlight. Use the shortcut key (one of the F-keys with the backlight icon) to toggle it or adjust brightness. If your key does not have the icon, the keyboard does not have a backlight (some regional variants may differ).

### Eye Comfort Mode

Built-in blue light filter. Right-click the desktop, go to Display settings, and enable Eye Comfort. The screen gets a slightly yellow tint. Useful for long sessions.

### F10 Factory Reset

The laptop has a built-in recovery feature:

1. Plug in the power adapter
2. Press the Power button, then repeatedly press or hold **F10** to enter recovery
3. Follow on-screen instructions

> This wipes the C: drive completely. Back up data first. Not available in all regions.

- **Shared clipboard**: Copy on phone, paste on laptop (and vice versa).
- **Automatic sync**: Phone files auto-sync to the laptop.
