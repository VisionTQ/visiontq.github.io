**Course Code:** ENG1103  
**Activity:** Theoretical (نظري)  
**Section:** 2  
**Credit Hours:** 3  
**Instructor:** Sameer Aliabri ([ssiabri@qu.edu.sa](mailto:ssiabri@qu.edu.sa))  
**Office Hours:** Wednesday 10-1  
**Semester:** 1st Semester 1448 H

## Textbook

Sandra N. Elbaum. 2021. *Grammar in Context 2.* (Latest edition available) Cengage Learning, Boston, Massachusetts.

## Course Outline

| Week | Topic |
|---|---|
| 1 | Introduction |
| 2-3 | **Chapter 1:** The Simple Present – Frequency Words |
| 4-5 | **Chapter 2:** The Present Continuous – The Future |
| 6-7 | **Chapter 3:** The Simple Past – The Habitual Past with Used To – Pronouns – Questions |
| 8 | **Midterm Exam** |
| 9-10 | **Chapter 4:** Possessive Forms – Objective Pronouns – Reflexive Pronouns – Questions |
| 11-12 | **Chapter 5:** Nouns – There + Be – Quantity Words |
| 13-14 | **Chapter 6:** Modifiers – Adverbs |
| 15-16 | **Chapter 7:** Time Words – The Past Continuous |

## Grading

- End-of-chapter quizzes: **10%**
- Spark Exercises: **10%**
- Midterm: **30%**
- Final: **50%**
