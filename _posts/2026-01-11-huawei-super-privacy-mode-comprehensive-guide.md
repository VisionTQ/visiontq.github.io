The main use for me is to prevent apps from secretly accessing your microphone, camera, or other sensors. TikTok is the biggest example for me. Sometimes I will talk about something, and suddenly I'm seeing videos or ads about exactly that. Definitely not a coincidence.

## How to Enable and Use It

### Step-by-Step Guide
1. Open **Settings** on your Huawei device
2. Go to the **Privacy & security**
3. Select **Super Privacy**
4. Tap **Turn on** (Depends on device type - foldable vs regular)

### Quick Way to Access Super Privacy
You can also add Super Privacy Mode to your Control Panel:
1. Swipe down from the top of your screen to open Control Panel
2. Tap the edit/pencil icon
3. Find and add the Super Privacy Mode toggle
4. Save your changes

Video tutorial:

Now you can enable/disable the feature with a single tap from your Control Panel!

## Features and Benefits

### Key Features 

| Feature                  | Description                                     |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------- |
| **Disables All Sensors** | Turns off microphone, camera, and other sensors |

### Supported Devices

**Operating Systems:**
- **HarmonyOS 4** and newer (China market devices)
- **EMUI 13** and newer (global market devices)

**Device Compatibility:**
- Most recent Huawei flagship devices
- Selected mid-range models with required hardware support

## Limitations

When Super Privacy Mode is active:
- **Face recognition** won't work
