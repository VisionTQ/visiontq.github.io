> Make sure your NPSSO cookie is fresh before using PSNTools. Sign out of PSN and sign back in if needed. [NPSSO guide here](/posts/how-to-get-your-npsso-token-for-playstation-api-access/).
{: .prompt-info }

## Step 1: Go to PSN Profile Modder

Head over to the [PSNTools homepage](https://www.psntools.com) and find the **PSN Profile Modder** section. Click it to get started.

![PSNTools homepage with PSN Profile Modder highlighted](/assets/psn-tools/profile-modder-homepage-highlighted.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

---

## Step 2: Reset Your Avatar

After clicking Profile Modder, it will ask you to load your profile.

 You can **reset your avatar to default**. This lets you get the default PlayStation avatar. The same one you get when creating a brand new account.

![PSN Profile Modder, reset avatar to default option](/assets/psn-tools/profile-modder-reset-avatar.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

---

## Step 3: Change Your Background Image

You can also change your **profile background image**. You will be able to browse your background history and pick from previous backgrounds you have used.

![PSN Profile Modder, change background image with history](/assets/psn-tools/profile-modder-background-image.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

---

## Step 4: Change Your Profile Background Color

You can change your **profile background color**. Pick any color you want to customize your profile look.

> This feature only works on PS4, not PS5.
{: .prompt-warning }

![PSN Profile Modder, change profile background color](/assets/psn-tools/profile-modder-background-color.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

---

## Step 5: Customize Your About Me

You can edit your **custom about me** section. Add symbols, special characters, and whatever text you want to show on your profile.

![PSN Profile Modder, customize about me with symbols](/assets/psn-tools/profile-modder-about-me.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

---

## Step 6: Customize Your Real Name

You can also customize your **real name** field. Add specific custom symbols and text to personalize how your name appears.

![PSN Profile Modder, customize real name with custom symbols](/assets/psn-tools/profile-modder-real-name.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

---
