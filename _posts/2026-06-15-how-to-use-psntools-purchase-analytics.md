## Step 1: Go to Purchase Analytics

Head over to the [Purchase Analytics page](https://www.psntools.com/psn/store/analytics). Click the **Select Date Range** button.

![PSN Purchase Analytics homepage with select date range button](/assets/psn-tools/purchase-analytics-date-range.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

---

## Step 2: Choose a Date Range

After clicking, you will see several options:
- Beginning of the PSN store
- Since 9 years ago
- Since 6 years ago
- Since 3 years ago
- Or set a **custom time** in the date section below

![Purchase Analytics date range options](/assets/psn-tools/purchase-analytics-date-options.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }

---

## Step 3: View Your Report

After selecting a date range, your store analytics report will show up. It includes how much you have spent on games, PSN Plus, & all kinds of purchases.

![Purchase Analytics report showing spending data](/assets/psn-tools/purchase-analytics-report.png){: .w-100 .shadow .rounded-10 }
